Planning Your Trip

When is the best time to visit Vietnam?

expand_more
Vietnam is a long country with three distinct climate zones. For the best weather nationwide, consider visiting from November to April. However, the lush greenery is most vibrant just after the rainy season in October.

What should I pack for a VNGroup Tourist tour?

expand_more
Lightweight, breathable clothing is essential. We recommend a mix of moisture-wicking fabrics, a sturdy pair of walking shoes, a light rain jacket, and modest attire for visiting temples (covering shoulders and knees).

Booking & Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

expand_more
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), bank transfers, and secure online payments via Stripe. For local bookings, we also facilitate MoMo and direct bank deposits.

Can I cancel my tour and get a refund?

expand_more
Yes, we offer flexible cancellation. Cancellations made 30 days prior to the tour receive a 100% refund (minus bank fees). Cancellations within 14-29 days receive a 50% refund.

Travel Safety

Do I need travel insurance?

expand_more
While not mandatory for entry, VNGroup Tourist highly recommends comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical emergencies and trip cancellations.

Is the tap water safe to drink in Vietnam?

expand_more
Tap water is generally not safe for drinking. We provide filtered water stations on all our tours and encourage guests to bring reusable bottles to minimize plastic waste.

Visa & Entry

How do I apply for a Vietnam E-Visa?

expand_more
You can apply directly through the official government portal. The process usually takes 3-5 business days. Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.
